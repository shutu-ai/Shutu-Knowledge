## Historical phase and transition audit

`TestDeriveHistoricalTimelineUsesMinorVersionPhases` verifies four ordered
minor-version phases, nonempty key changes, exact source units, and evidence
for every phase transition. `TestDeriveHistoricalTimelineUsesMajorPhases`
verifies three evidence-backed major-era phases. Ambiguous ranges remain
unmaterialized rather than being converted to a fabricated boundary.
