# FlowPump X200 User Manual

## Specifications

The FlowPump X200 delivers a maximum flow rate of 42 L/min at 60 Hz.
Noise output is 58 dB(A) at one meter under nominal load.
The inlet connector accepts 25 mm hoses. Operating temperature range is 5 to 45 degrees Celsius.

## Quick start

Prime the pump before the first start of the day. The priming plug is located on top of the housing.
Never run the pump dry for longer than 30 seconds.

## Alarms

When alarm E-201 is shown, the inlet screen is blocked and the pump reduces speed automatically.
Clear the inlet screen before restarting.
